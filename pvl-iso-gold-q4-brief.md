# PVL ISO GOLD | Q4/2026 TikTok Campaign Brief
**Brand:** Ultimate Sup (Singapore) | **Audience:** Gym-goers aged 18–35

## 0. Implementation Process
| Step | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product audit (page, label, price) | Fact base |
| 2 | Market scan (participation, category size) | Opportunity |
| 3 | Competitor benchmark (ON Isolate, Dymatize ISO100, regular whey as price anchor) | Gaps |
| 4 | Pain mining (clinical + industry data; validate with comment mining) | Pain list |
| 5 | Map USP ↔ Pain ↔ Message ↔ Scene | Logic chain |
| 6 | Script + A/B test plan | Brief |

**Context:** Singaporeans exercising weekly rose from 54% (2015) to 73% (2023) [1]. Sports nutrition is ~US$515M (2025) → US$802M (2033), 5.7% CAGR, with protein powder the leading product type [2]. Q4 brings 11.11 / 12.12 sale peaks.

## 1. Unique Selling Points
| # | USP | Evidence | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **27g grass-fed whey isolate** per serving | Product page | Rivals ≈25g [3][4] (+8%); inside ISSN 20–40g dose range [5] |
| 2 | **Gut-first formula:** added enzymes + 1B CFU DE111 probiotic | Product page; DE111 trials are small and supplier-funded [6] | Rivals headline purity, not gut support (verify labels) |
| 3 | **Zero filler,** no artificial colours/flavours, no aspartame, "Clean · Tested · Approved" mark | Pack/page | Answers label distrust |
| 4 | **13 flavour/size options,** 6g natural BCAAs | Product page | Lowers trial risk |
| 5 | **Easy checkout:** Atome 3×S$30.53, 10% off first order, free shipping ≥S$99 | Storefront | Softens price |

**Weakness:** S$91.60/2lb ≈ S$3.2 per serving (est., ~31g scoop [7]) vs regular whey 2lb at S$54.63 [8]. Answer with value, don't hide it.

## 2. Target Audience Pain Points
| # | Pain | Data | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Protein bloats me" | 83–91% of Malay/Chinese/Indian adults hypolactasic (Penang, n=100/group), the closest proxy; no Singapore study found [9] | Biggest barrier for Singapore's ethnic mix; isolate + enzymes + probiotic is the answer |
| 2 | "Can I trust the tub?" | ~25% of products miss label claims (LGC, interested party); amino spiking passes nitrogen tests [10] | Proof beats promises |
| 3 | "Is S$90 worth it?" | S$91.60 vs S$54.63 regular whey | Needs value framing + split payment |

*Gap:* No Singapore consumer survey found. Validate by mining comments on 50 competitor TikToks.

## 3. Key Messages
| # | Message | Pain | Proof | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (hero) | **"27g that sits right."** | 1 | Isolate + enzymes + DE111 | Say "formulated for easier digestion"; never "cures bloating" |
| 2 | **"27g. Zero filler. Nothing to hide."** | 2 | Clean label, tested mark | Confirm certifier |
| 3 | **"Premium, made easy."** | 3 | Atome, 10% off, free shipping | Q4 frame: *End 2026 stronger* |

## 4. TikTok Script: "The Shake That Doesn't Fight Back"
**Spec:** 9:16, 35s, creator UGC, voiceover + captions, #ad disclosure.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / On-screen | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator post-gym, hand on stomach, shaker in hand | "Protein shake, then my stomach hates me. Sound familiar?" / **"Gym bloat? 🙋"** | Hook (Pain 1) |
| 3–8s | Regular tubs crossed out | "Most whey is built for protein. Not for your gut." | Agitate |
| 8–16s | Scoop PVL, label close-ups | "PVL Iso Gold: 27g grass-fed isolate, plus enzymes and 1 billion probiotics." / **27g · Enzymes · 1B DE111** | Message 1 |
| 16–22s | Shake pour, taste reaction, flavour lineup | "Zero filler. No artificial colours. 13 flavours, and Triple Milk Choco is my pick." | Message 2 |
| 22–28s | Creator lifting, relaxed | "Now post-workout is the best part of my day." / *Individual results vary* | Social proof |
| 28–35s | Product page, price, Atome | "S$91.60, or 3 payments of S$30.53 with Atome. 10% off your first order. Tap the basket." / **Free shipping from S$99: add a shaker** | CTA (Message 3) |

**Caption:** Shake that sits right 💛 27g isolate + enzymes + probiotic. #sgfitness #wheyprotein #gymsg #ultimatesup #1212

**A/B hooks:** (A) pain "stomach hates me", (B) stat "27g vs 25g", (C) price "3×S$30.53".
**KPIs:** 3s view rate, 6s hold, CTR, add-to-cart, CVR, ROAS, AOV (shaker bundle to cross S$99). Set targets from account baseline.
